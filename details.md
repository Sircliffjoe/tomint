Introduction
It is generally acknowledged that the adolescent period of life is the stage of character and personality formation.

A sure way to raise a godly generation is to build their character and personality around Christ, the Solid Rock during adolescent years.

Where our parents and even the church are “too busy” and ignorant to do this, the devil is always pleased to lend a hand. Our teens are under more pressure than ever to get involved with drugs, premarital sex, alcohol, the occult, false religion and the like. Thank God for establishing the Teenagers' Outreach Ministries (TOM) Inc. is an interdenominational / non-denominational, non-sectarian, charitable Christian organization with a focus on the spiritual, physical, physical, social and intellectual welfare of teenagers. In line with I Tim. 4:12 “Let no man despise your youth, but be an example to the believers...” TOM is out to make today's' teenager an exemplary person.

About Teenager Outreach Ministries (TOM):
Teenager Outreach Ministries (TOM) is not a church denomination (and does not intend to be one) but is out to serve and work together with churches, fellowships, parents and other Christian organizations.

History:
The ministry started on July 1, 1992 as a result of a vision given to Ihezie, O.U.Imo (The President). Tom has her international headquarters in Kwali, federal capital territory (FCT) Abuja, Nigeria. We have established our presence in over twenty – four (24) states in Nigeria and a few other countries.
TOM is financed through offerings, tithes, donations, and gifts from individuals, churches, organizations, and self-help projects.

Our Vision:
To share the gospel of Christ with teenagers of all nations as well disciple them for the Lord Jesus Christ and unto maturity in the Christian faith.

Our Conviction:
Is that through a flexible and dynamic fellowship devoid of denominational traditions such as the teenagers’ club, we can equip today’s Teenagers to overcome all sorts of negative pressures present in our society today, enabling them to be exemplary Christians.

Our Strategy:
Among other supportive programs, is to establish a Teenagers club in every neighborhood, making it possible for every Teenager to have access to a Christ sharing, loving and lively fellowship (psalm 16;11b) tailored to meet their need.

OUR PROGRAMMES:
We are fulfilling this vision through three (3) major strategies: Discipleship, Outreach & Trainings

1. DISCIPLESHIP PROGRAMMES
These are programmes aimed at getting our teens to become Christ centred and balanced adults. They include:

a. Bible teachings/discussions also occasional retreats through the clubs.

b. Teenagers' Bible Institute to be run from time to time.

c. Exposure to missions/social work projects.

d. Social activities: Games/ Outreach Parties, picnics, Christmas dinners, etc.

e. The TomMAg: our quarterly magazine through which we minister to teens. This has been of tremendous blessing to our teens.


2. OUTREACH PROGRAMS
These are programmes designed primarily to share the gospel with teenagers. They include:

a. Teenagers' Clubs: this is our main strategy. At present, we have about sixty-one (61) clubs in different parts of the country. We have assisted many churches to establish clubs for their teens and others within the neighbourhood. These clubs, through their two (2) hours weekly meetings, strive to reach the teens around them. Using teens to reach their peers have proven very effective.

b. Vacation camps: we hold six (6) days camps both within and outside Nigeria. God has given us great testimonies and many lives have been touched. Thousands of teens are reached every year through these camps. The number continues to grow yearly.

c. Seminars in schools: these are outreach programmes to secondary schools focussing on teen's sexuality, crime, drugs and peer pressure, etc. We have seen so many embrace Christ and chastity through these outreaches.

d. Reformatory homes: the number of Christian parents who seem unable to explain their teen's delinquent behaviour is growing. A home for such teens would be established at different locations across the country to minster to them and other delinquent teens. The first home will be set up at our National Camp Site, Abuja.


3. TRAINING PROGRAMME
TOM lays a lot of emphasis on training. No one can do well, what he or she has not been trained to do. As a result, we hold regular training for all our fulltime and volunteer staff, youth workers and parents. These include:

a. TOM National Teenagers' Workers Convention: International and experienced teen workers and ministers are invited to further equip and challenge teenagers' workers.

Panel 2: Training (Cont.), Mission, & State Address
b. TOM Certificate Course in Teenagers' Ministry: For intending club leaders, youth pastors, parents, etc. Over five thousand (5,000) people have attended this course so far and testimonies abound!

c. Teen Handlers' Seminar: A six-hour one day seminar to further strengthen and sharpen the skills of teenagers' workers.

d. Seminar for Parents: We have been privileged to share this seminar in a number of churches across the country. Our book “Parents and Teenagers – Towards a Better Relationship", our main text for these seminars has been a blessing to many families.

e. Seminar for Christian Secondary School Teachers: Teachers spend more time with students than any other person. This seminar equips Christian teachers to make a positive and lasting impact in the lives of the students through their daily interactions with them.
